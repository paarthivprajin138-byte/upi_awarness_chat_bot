UPI Safety Coach

A student project to help spot suspicious payment situations

Safety first: Never type a real UPI PIN, OTP, password, card number, or bank account number here.
If money may have been stolen in India, contact your bank immediately and call 1930 as soon as possible. You can also report at cybercrime.gov.in. This educational prototype uses keyword matching and may be wrong; verify advice with your bank or a trusted adult.
